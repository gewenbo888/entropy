# ENTROPY · 熵 — The Ultimate Direction of the Universe

A monumental, museum-grade, bilingual (EN ⇄ 中文) cinematic web experience about **entropy** — the one law that gives time a direction and the universe a fate. A digital cathedral spanning thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, information theory, cosmology, life, black holes, artificial intelligence, and the heat death of everything.

> *The universe remembers order only by destroying it.*
> *宇宙通过毁灭秩序来记忆时间。*

## Experience

Nine cinematic chapters, each with deep bilingual writing, glowing equations, and a live interactive simulation:

1. **Hero** — monumental liquid-metal title over a GPU cosmic field; the cursor lenses spacetime.
2. **What Is Entropy** — drag heat into an ordered lattice and watch a live Shannon-entropy meter climb (`EntropyBox`); Clausius + Boltzmann equations; eight domains of one law.
3. **Entropy & Time** — scrub a cosmic clock from the Big Bang to the Heat Death (`UniverseTimeline`); quotes from Boltzmann, Einstein, Prigogine, Schrödinger, Shannon.
4. **Information Entropy** — type text and compute its Shannon entropy in real time (`InfoEntropy`): predictability, randomness, symbol spectrum.
5. **Life Against Entropy** — a self-organizing neural field condenses order out of noise (`LifeField`); negentropy, dissipative structures, consciousness.
6. **Black Holes** — feed stars into a black hole and watch the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy climb (`BlackHoleSim`); maximum-entropy objects, Hawking radiation.
7. **AI & Digital Entropy** — an endlessly self-evolving glyph automaton (`AIUniverse`); hallucination, synthetic reality, the collapse of meaning.
8. **The Heat Death** — stars extinguish one by one into the dark (`HeatDeathField`); the Degenerate, Black-Hole, and Dark Eras.
9. **Final** — the interface dissolves into thermal noise, the cursor becomes stardust, and only a faint cosmic background remains: *Entropy was never destruction. It was transformation.*

A floating **中文 / EN** toggle cross-fades the entire site's language while every animation keeps running.

## Stack

- **Next.js 14** (App Router, `src/app/`) + **React 18** + **TypeScript** (strict)
- **TailwindCSS** for the design system
- **Three.js** — GPU particle cosmos with a custom GLSL gravitational-lensing shader
- **Framer Motion** — language cross-fades, scroll-driven parallax, reveals
- **HTML5 Canvas 2D** — six bespoke interactive scientific simulations
- Fonts: **Cinzel** (monumental display), **Cormorant Garamond** (serif), **Space Grotesk** (UI), **JetBrains Mono** (equations), **Noto Serif SC** (中文)
